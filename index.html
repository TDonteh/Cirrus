How the AI layer works

Every device on BittPulse does the same three things, just for different rooms of your life.

01

Read continuously

Particle counters, HRV sensors, and thermistors sample the environment or body every few seconds, not once a day.

02

Model the pattern

On-device models learn your baseline — a normal Tuesday air quality, a normal REM cycle — so they can flag what's actually different.

03

Adjust before you ask

Fan speed ramps up before you smell smoke. A mattress cools before your temperature spikes. The app tells you why, after the fact.
